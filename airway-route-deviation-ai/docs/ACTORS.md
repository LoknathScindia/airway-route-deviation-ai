# System Actors

## Primary Actors

### Airline Operations Manager

Responsibilities

- Monitor active flights
- Review deviation predictions
- Evaluate alternate routes
- Monitor operational risks

---

### Flight Dispatcher

Responsibilities

- Analyze flight plans
- Review weather conditions
- Approve alternate routes

---

### Aviation Analyst

Responsibilities

- Analyze historical flight data
- Evaluate model performance
- Generate reports

---

## Secondary Actors

### Weather Service

Provides

- Wind information
- Storm information
- Visibility
- Temperature

---

### Flight Data Provider

Provides

- Flight trajectory
- Aircraft position
- Speed
- Altitude

---

### Airspace Information Provider

Provides

- Restricted airspace
- NOTAMs
- Airway information
