# AeroMind AI

## Vision

To build an intelligent aviation decision support platform capable of predicting airway route deviations before they occur, explaining the reasons behind the prediction, and recommending optimized flight routes that improve safety, reduce delays, minimize fuel consumption, and lower carbon emissions.

---

## Mission

Provide airlines and aviation operators with an AI-powered platform that transforms flight, weather, and airspace data into actionable operational insights.

---

## Target Users

- Airline Operations Centers
- Flight Dispatchers
- Air Traffic Analysts
- Aviation Researchers
- Airport Authorities

---

## Core Objectives

- Predict route deviations
- Explain predictions using Explainable AI
- Recommend optimal alternate routes
- Estimate fuel consumption
- Estimate CO₂ emissions
- Visualize flight trajectories
- Monitor operational risk
