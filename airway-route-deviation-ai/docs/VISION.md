# Airway Route Deviation Prediction System

## Product Name

AeroMind AI

---

# Vision

To develop an AI-powered aviation decision support platform capable of predicting airway route deviations before they occur, identifying the reasons behind the deviation, and recommending safer and more efficient flight routes.

The platform aims to improve flight safety, reduce operational delays, optimize fuel consumption, and minimize environmental impact.

---

# Problem Statement

Unexpected flight route deviations occur due to weather conditions, air traffic congestion, restricted airspace, emergencies, and operational constraints.

Current systems often detect deviations only after they begin rather than predicting them in advance.

This project aims to provide early warnings and intelligent route recommendations using artificial intelligence and aviation data.

---

# Objectives

• Predict flight route deviations.

• Explain why a deviation is likely.

• Recommend alternate routes.

• Estimate fuel savings.

• Estimate CO₂ reduction.

• Provide an interactive visualization dashboard.

---

# Target Users

- Airline Operations Centers
- Flight Dispatchers
- Air Traffic Analysts
- Aviation Researchers
- Airport Authorities
