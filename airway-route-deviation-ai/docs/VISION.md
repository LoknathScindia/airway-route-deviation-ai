# Airway Route Deviation Prediction System

## Product Name

Flight

---

# Vision

To develop an AI-powered flight decision support system capable of predicting airway route deviations before they occur, identifying the reasons behind potential deviations, and recommending safer and more efficient flight routes.

The system aims to improve flight safety, reduce operational delays, optimize fuel consumption, and minimize environmental impact through intelligent predictive analytics.

---

# Problem Statement

Unexpected flight route deviations occur due to weather conditions, air traffic congestion, restricted airspace, emergencies, and other operational constraints.

Traditional systems often detect deviations only after they have already begun, limiting the ability of airline operators to take proactive action.

This project provides early warnings, predictive insights, and intelligent alternate route recommendations using artificial intelligence and aviation data.

---

# Objectives

- Predict potential flight route deviations before they occur.
- Identify the factors contributing to route deviations.
- Recommend safer and more efficient alternate routes.
- Estimate potential fuel savings.
- Estimate potential CO₂ emission reductions.
- Provide an interactive analytics and visualization dashboard.
- Support data-driven decision making for flight operations.
